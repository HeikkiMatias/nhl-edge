# Debutants' NHLe in their debut season, debut-nhle-20261029-643cc58

The evidence for ADR 0050 (#125), from `scripts/debut_nhle.py`: RAPM's 5v5 model fitted three times with the frozen settings (`rapm.TUNED`) on the training seasons, scored on 2012-13 to 2017-18 (ADR 0011). Intervals are 95% weekly block bootstrap (hard rule 7).

- **base (today):** a debutant's traits are fixed at the season's start, before his lines are public (ADR 0016), so his prior has no NHLe all season (ADR 0020).
- **a1, the prior:** from the first as-of time after his lines are public, his prior mean reads his NHLe, with the season's trait effects and NHLe factors held fixed.
- **a2, the prior and the rows:** a1, and his rows of the season carry his NHLe in the trait columns, which only later seasons' season-start fits read.

The base fit reproduces the lake's player_ratings (rapm-20261003-86a736f): 737,960 of 737,960 5v5 ratings, at most 6e-15 apart.

## Predictions

Each measure is the run's minus base's, negative when the run predicts better:
- **Win log loss:** the home team's full-game win, from each game's projected 5v5 xG difference through a win model fitted on the earlier seasons (the tuning's measure, ADR 0011).
- **5v5 xG difference:** the squared error of the game's realized 5v5 xG difference, predicted by a line fitted on the earlier seasons.
- **5v5 stints:** each game's own 5v5 rows, predicted by the fit that rates the game, before they are read: RAPM's own loss, its hours-weighted squared error, per hour. The second line keeps the rows with a debutant on the ice whose lines were public before the game.
- **Slope:** the regression of base's stint residual on the run's change of prediction, hours-weighted, through 0: 1 when the change moves the predictions as far as the data says, 0 when it carries no information.
